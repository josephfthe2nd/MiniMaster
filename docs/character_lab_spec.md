# Character Lab — synthesized build spec

## Chosen approach
MERGED DESIGN — skeleton = Design 4 (one connected closed body cage: 16-column phase-offset torso, a SINGLE crotch quad that splits the pelvis ring into two 8-vert leg loops, and one 2x2-quad armhole patch per side bridged to an 8-vert arm tube), upgraded with: Design 1's transported-frame rule for limb rings (e2 = t x e1, so one formula winds both sides correctly), Design 2's protrude-recess-protrude 3-ring lip sandwich and thumb bump, Design 3's fixed atom-application order and drive-resolution composites, and connected MITTEN HANDS (D1/D2/D3) instead of D4's separate glove shells. Two closed quad cages + two icosphere eyeballs: (1) BODY, one connected genus-0 cage, 403 verts / 401 quads (chi = 403 - 802 + 401 = 2, verified), zero seams anywhere on the body including ankles and wrists; (2) HEAD, a separate 16-column x 11-ring loft_rings shell (178 verts / 176 quads, chi=2) with a TRUE front midline column for the nose-bridge/philtrum/chin line, overlapping the body's capped neck stub. WHY D4's crotch: one quad, zero added verts, valence-5 at four R0 verts (CC-legal), and I re-derived every directed edge — it cancels exactly against the band quads and leg bridges; D1's perineum-vert bottom-face and D3's saddle both work but carry more special-case index math for no visual gain. WHY the separate head (honest tradeoff): the face needs a midline vertex column and 13-degree column spacing at the front; welding it into the phase-offset torso (which must NOT have midline verts, for the crotch quad) forces a column-count transition band — fragile topology for an invisible benefit. The seam is an overlapping-shell intersection tucked under the jaw: head neck-stub (radius 0.026H) sits inside the body neck tube (0.042H), body neck-cap pole (z=0.87H) sits inside the skull; the crease reads as jawline shadow in gouraud and vanishes in STL (mesh.py documents slicer union of overlapping watertight shells). WHY connected mitten hands over D4's glove shells: two fewer shells, no cuff crease, hand_size is a scale about the wrist ring — strictly simpler. WHY 11-ring/16-col head over D2's 14x20: D2's head alone would be 280 cage quads (4480 at subdiv 2) — it blows ~half the live budget for detail gouraud cannot show at 30mm; the 3-ring lip stack is the one D2 feature that actually buys readability, so it is kept. TOPOLOGY IS CONSTANT: quads, skin-weight matrix, feature-group index tables, and the orphan-compaction remap are built once at import; the 38 sliders drive vertex POSITIONS only. Rejected over-promises: D4's separate ear pancake shells and 'flush invisible contact' (replaced by ear vertex-bump groups — zero shells, zero seam risk); D3's tanh soft-clamp (replaced by hard clip with 25% headroom — monotone, explainable slider response); any thumb-as-topology (D1 proves a 4-ring cap tube can't stay all-quad; thumb is a 2-vert displacement bump).

## Body cage
UNITS/FRAME: Z-up, character faces -Y, +X = figure's left. All coordinates are fractions of H (total height; mm scale applied last). Radii authored ~1.15x proud of target silhouette to absorb Catmull-Clark shrinkage (single global constant CC_INFLATE=1.15, already folded into the numbers below; calibrate once, never per-ring).

== CROTCH (crux #1): ONE quad ==
Q_crotch = [id(0,12), id(0,11), id(0,4), id(0,3)]. Verified: its edges 12->11 and 4->3 are the reverses of band-0 quads' R0 traversals (11->12, 3->4); edges 11->4 and 3->12 are the two inseams, each consumed once (reversed) by a leg-loop closing bridge quad below. == ARM HOLES (crux #2): 2x2 patch removal, rim derived by directed-edge cancellation ==
LEFT rim (verified vert-by-vert; every rim edge below is traversed opposite to its surviving band-quad neighbor): B_left = [id(5,15), id(5,0), id(5,1), id(6,1), id(7,1), id(7,0), id(7,15), id(6,15)] (bottom front->back, up the back, top back->front, down the front). RIGHT rim: B_right = [id(5,7), id(5,8), id(5,9), id(6,9), id(7,9), id(7,8), id(7,7), id(6,7)] (bottom back->front, up the front, top front->back, down the back — the mirror reversal is REQUIRED to keep orientation). Bridge (identical formula everywhere): [Rim_m, Rim_{(m+1)%8}, Inner_{(m+1)%8}, Inner_m] for m=0..7, Inner = first limb ring (S0 or L0). Legs use A_left/A_right as Rim with L0 as Inner.

== LIMB RING FRAME RULE (one formula, both sides, no winding flips ever) ==
Every limb ring: P_m = c + r1*cos(a_m)*e1 + r2*sin(a_m)*e2 with a right-handed transported frame; e2 = t x e1 where t = unit travel direction AWAY from the body — because e2 flips sign with t, the same alpha table winds both sides outward. Bands: [Rj_m, Rj_{m+1}, R{j+1}_{m+1}, R{j+1}_m] with j increasing away from the body. End cap (fingertip/toe): [pole, last_m, last_{m+1}, last_{(m+2)%8}] for m=0,2,4,6. Right limbs are REBUILT from mirrored center/azimuth tables — never position-mirror finished quads.
ARMS: e1=(0,0,-1) (down), t=+X (left) / -X (right), so e2=+Y (left) / -Y (right); a_m = 45*(m-1) deg. Check: left m=0 -> down-front, matches B_left[0]=id(5,15) (bottom-front); right m=0 -> down-back, matches B_right[0]=id(5,7) (bottom-back). Ring frames rotate about e2 as travel bends from outboard (+/-X at S0) to downward (-Z from S2 on); transport continuously, never flip.
LEGS: rings horizontal, marching DOWN; azimuth delta_m = (-22.5 + 45*m) deg measured from +Y (back) toward +X-outer for the LEFT leg: P_m = c + (r1*sin(delta_m)*out, r2*cos(delta_m), dz) where out=+1 left / -1 right. This is CW-seen-from-+Z, which with top-down band marching equals loft's outward convention (verified: bridge quads consume A_left forward, band quads consume each lower ring forward). m=0 back-inner matches A_left[0]=id(0,3). Foot rings rotate the frame -90 deg about the out-axis... i.e. about X so ring normals swing from +Z-march to -Y-march through the ankle; vertex correspondence m is preserved (no seam at the ankle).

## Composites
DRIVE RESOLUTION (single code path; composites are literally weighted sums of atomic effects and NEVER move the user's stored slider values): eff[x] = clip(user[x] + sum(coupling_terms), 1.25*lo_x, 1.25*hi_x) computed in one pre-pass; all vertex math consumes ONLY effective atomics. Plain np.clip with 25% headroom — NOT tanh (decision vs Design 3: soft-clamp makes slider response non-monotone-feeling and hard to reason about; the last 10% of stacked extremes saturates instead, documented in the UI, Fallout-style).
GENDER g in [0,1], gm = 2g-1: the 16 couplings listed on the gender row of the param table (shoulder/hip/waist ratios + raised female waist ring, bust, muscle baseline, neck, jaw, brow, chin width, lips, cheek height+fullness, face width, glutes, deltoid radius). At g=0 this reproduces anatomy.md's male column (sh/hip ~1.4, heavy jaw/brow, flat chest); at g=1 the female column (hips >= shoulders, high narrow waist, soft jaw, fuller lips/cheeks); g=0.5 is a true androgyne and every atomic slider stays live on top.
WEIGHT w in [-1,1]: proximal/truncal fat map (param-table row) — belly/love-handles strongest, hips/glutes/thighs/upper-arms next, extremities barely; face couplings cheek_full += 0.45w + double-chin atom. Negative runs every atom in reverse (gaunt: visible waist pinch, sunken cheeks, thin limbs). Flesh only — joints never move with weight.
MUSCLE m in [-1,1]: 1:1 with anatomy.md par.4 masses (param-table row): deltoid cap, biceps front-upper, triceps back, forearm bulk proximal, pecs, traps (fills the R7->R8 slope band), lats, glutes, quads front-biased, gastrocnemius high-and-back, waist firming. Negative = atrophied (skeleton-adjacent). Pinch rings excluded.
SHARED DRIVES: glute_drive = clip(0.5*eff_muscle + 0.5*weight + 0.25*max(gm,0), -1.25, 1.25) feeds the R1 glute atom; bust_drive = clip(bust + 0.8*max(gm,0), 0, 1.25).
ORTHOGONALITY RULES (enforced by construction, from Design 3): (1) scale-type sliders (widths, lengths, ring multipliers) edit RING PARAMETERS before vertex placement; displacement atoms are absolute adds after — so any two sliders commute; (2) atoms use disjoint core vertex sets where anatomically possible; where they overlap (cheek_full vs cheek_depth) they use orthogonal directions (radial vs malar-normal); (3) mouth_width pins c0 while lip_full is c0-centered; eye_spacing translates the center that eye_size scales about, evaluated center-first.
FIXED APPLICATION ORDER (deterministic): resolve drives -> joint layout -> ring-param table -> place ring verts -> additive atom pass (one vectorized sum over the sparse atom basis) -> head_size/world placement -> LBS -> mm scale.

## Skinning
LBS on CAGE verts, THEN subdivide — CC rounds the coarse 3-ring LBS crease into an organic bend (the architecture's contract). BONE-KEY SEMANTICS (armature.py): bone key = CHILD joint name; skin_matrices['knee_l'] = W_hip_l @ T(-rest_hip_l), i.e. binding to bone J applies the rotation of J's PARENT joint. Identity at rest. Joint layout: EXACT names/parenting from build_humanoid (pelvis, spine, chest, neck, head_top, shoulder/elbow/wrist/hand_{l,r}, hip/knee/ankle/toe_{l,r}) so humanoid_poses() applies verbatim; positions canon-aligned: pelvis (0,0,0.515H), spine (0,0,0.62H), chest (0,0,0.72H), neck (0,0,0.80H), head_top (0,0,chin_z+Hh); shoulder (+-0.094H*sh_eff, 0, 0.78H); elbow (+-0.100H, 0, 0.62H); wrist (+-0.106H, 0, 0.50H); hand (+-0.106H, 0, 0.455H); hip (+-0.045H, 0, 0.50H) MEDIAL per anatomy par.3/6; knee (+-0.043H, 0, 0.26H); ankle (+-0.040H, 0, 0.055H); toe (+-0.040H, -0.085H*foot_size, 0.02H). Length sliders move joints and rings from the same formulas.
WEIGHT TABLE W (constant (403 x nb) float64 built once at import; rows sum to 1; max 2 bones/vert; each hinge gets exactly ONE 50/50 ring flanked by fully-bound rings ~0.06-0.10H away):
TORSO — R0 (incl. all crotch-quad verts), R1: spine 1.0. R2: spine 0.5 / chest 0.5 (waist bend). R3, R4: chest 1.0. R5, R6, R7: neck 1.0 (i.e. follows the chest joint — shoulders ride the chest) EXCEPT the 8 armhole rim verts per side: neck 0.7 / elbow_side 0.3 (deltoid drags trapezius flesh on arm raises; this is what makes the shoulder bend read). R8: neck 0.5 / head_top 0.5. NECK_POLE: head_top 1.0.
ARMS (side s) — S0: neck 0.4 / elbow_s 0.6. S1, S2: elbow_s 1.0. S3 (elbow ring): elbow_s 0.5 / wrist_s 0.5. S4: wrist_s 1.0. S5 (wrist ring): wrist_s 0.5 / hand_s 0.5. S6, S7, pole: hand_s 1.0.
LEGS (side s) — L0: knee_s 0.7 / spine 0.3 (hip crease; thigh root drags pelvis flesh). L1: knee_s 1.0. L2 (knee ring): knee_s 0.5 / ankle_s 0.5. L3, L4: ankle_s 1.0. L5 (ankle ring): ankle_s 0.5 / toe_s 0.5. F0, F1, toe pole: toe_s 1.0.
HEAD shell + eyeballs: head_top 1.0 (rigid — facial geometry is morph-driven; the neck crease hides in the shell overlap). Eyeballs transformed rigidly by M['head_top'], no per-vert blend.
EVALUATION: gather the ~14 used bone matrices into B (nb,4,4); posed = einsum('vb,bij,vj->vi', W, B[:,:3,:], homog(v)) — one einsum per shell, <0.1 ms at 403 verts. Pose sanity vs humanoid_poses(): elbow -70, knee 42 bend at the single 50/50 rings and smooth out at subdiv 1; shoulder -130 (attack/rage) stretches the bridge band exactly where a deltoid deforms — the rim verts' 0.3 elbow weight prevents armpit tearing; no twist DOFs in the shipped pose set, so no candy-wrapper. If the armpit collapses at -130, the fix is weight-table-only: move S0 to 0.5/0.5 and give S1 0.15 neck — never topology.

## Pipeline
character(params: dict, pose_name: str | None, levels: int) -> Mesh. PURE and deterministic: float64 throughout, fixed atom order, no RNG — identical params+pose = byte-identical mesh.
IMPORT-TIME CONSTANTS (topology never changes): BODY_QUADS (401,4) from build_topology() (torso bands minus 8 hole quads, neck cap, crotch quad, 4x[bridge+bands+cap], then orphan compaction remap of ids 96/104); HEAD_QUADS (176,4) from loft_rings on a dummy ring stack (indices only); W (403 x nb) skin matrix; all feature-group (idx, w) arrays; the sparse atom basis; rim/loop orderings. Unit tests assert chi=2 per shell and every vertex referenced, once.
PER EDIT/FRAME: (1) d = resolve(params) — composite arithmetic, ~40 floats; (2) J = joint_layout(d) -> Armature (existing add_joint API, same names/parents; rebuilt in ~1e-4 s); (3) body_v = body_verts(d, J) (403x3: ring tables + atom pass), head_v, eye_centers, eye_r = head_verts(d, J); (4) M = arm.skin_matrices(humanoid_poses()[pose_name] if pose_name else None); body_v = einsum-LBS(body_v, W, M); head_v/eyes rigid via M['head_top']; (5) subdivide_quads(body_v, BODY_QUADS, levels) and (head_v, HEAD_QUADS, levels); eyeballs are icosphere tri meshes already; (6) per-shell gate: quads_to_mesh(...).integrity_report() watertight AND volume>0 (body, head, eye_l, eye_r separately — matching mesh.py's per-shell-then-union export contract), then Mesh.merge -> render or STL; (7) mm scale by height folded into step 3 (verts and joints together).
PERF: live tkinter drag runs levels=1: 401+176 cage quads -> 1604+704 = 2308 quads / ~4.6k tris + 2x80-tri eyeballs; the whole rebuild is ~600 cage verts of arithmetic, one einsum, one vectorized _cc_once (a handful of np.add.at scatters) — well under 10 ms, comfortably interactive. levels=2 (6416+2816 quads, ~18.5k tris) runs on mouse-up and for export/beauty renders, cached until the next param change. Face-only slider changes may skip body_verts entirely (shells independent until merge) — optional, still pure.

## Ranked risks
1. 1. Winding/index slips at the 3 hand-authored junctions (2 armholes + crotch) — the #1 implementation hazard. Mitigation: the rim orders in this spec were re-derived by directed-edge cancellation against every surviving band quad (not copied from the designs); _cc_once's manifold gate catches any topology error on the first call; CI asserts per-shell chi=2 and integrity_report watertight+volume>0 at levels 0/1/2 for default, all-lo, all-hi params. A flipped strip is a one-line loop reversal, never a redesign.
2. 2. Orphan verts NaN the subdivider — ids 96 (R6_0) and 104 (R6_8) MUST go through the compaction remap; _cc_once divides by valence and a valence-0 vert NaNs the whole mesh silently downstream. Test asserts np.unique(quads).size == len(verts).
3. 3. Catmull-Clark shrinkage flattens features and thins wrists/ankles/nose — all radii pre-inflated by the single CC_INFLATE=1.15 constant and every face delta >= 0.02Hh (most >= 0.03Hh). Calibrate the constant ONCE against target silhouette widths (e.g. rendered hip width vs 2*0.104H); if the subdiv-2 face reads mushy, scale the delta tables — never densify head topology (head is already ~30% of the live quad budget).
4. 4. Head/body neck seam — an overlapping-shell intersection crease is visible in gouraud (invisible in printed STL per mesh.py's union contract). Parked under the jaw where a crease reads as jawline; head r0 radii are coupled to neck_thick so the overlap never opens a gap; builder asserts overlap depth > 0.01H at the worst corner (neck_len=1.4, head_size=0.85, neck_thick=0.75). Fallback if ever objectionable: weld via a 16->16 column transition band (possible since both are 16-col), at the cost of face/torso slider coupling — explicitly deferred, not v1.
5. 5. LBS armpit collapse at shoulder -130 deg (attack/rage) — 2-bone blends on a coarse cage pinch at extremes. Acceptable for 30mm minis; the rim verts' 0.3 elbow weight and S0's 0.4 neck weight absorb most of it; documented weight-table-only fix (S0 -> 0.5/0.5, S1 += 0.15 neck) if visual QA objects. No topology change permitted for this.
6. 6. Slider-extreme self-intersection (nose_length + lip_full both +1; eye_depth -1 + eye_size +1 poking the brow; hips 1.3 + weight 1 folding the crotch quad) — ranges chosen so max summed deltas stay under half the inter-column spacing; clip happens in resolve() ONLY, never in vertex math; the pairwise-extreme sweep test renders the known-bad corners; lip ring z-order asserted and proportionally scaled back if violated.
7. 7. Composite saturation — gender=1 + hips=1.3 exceeds atomic headroom and clips at 1.25x range, so at gender extremes some sliders lose part of their range. Same behavior as Fallout; document in the UI tooltip rather than fight it with nonlinear remaps.
8. 8. Mitten hands / bump ears / no nostrils-philtrum-eyelids — honest stylization fences that read correctly at 30mm with gouraud but disappoint at face-filling zoom. Scope fences, not bugs; the thumb-as-topology follow-up is known-hard (a 4-ring cap tube cannot stay all-quad) and explicitly out of v1.
9. 9. Leg-ring azimuth shear at the inner columns — the torso loop has only 2 inner-side verts vs the leg ring's uniform 45-deg spacing, so the bridge band shears ~45 deg near the inseam. Monotone same-direction correspondence guarantees no twist; CC smooths the shear; if the inner thigh dimples, bias delta_m by +11.25 deg — a constant tweak, not index math.
10. 10. Perf trap: running levels=2 during drag (~18.5k tris) would drop the tkinter painter below interactive — the pipeline pins live view to levels=1 with a levels=2 upgrade on mouse-up, and the level-2 result is cached until the next param change.
